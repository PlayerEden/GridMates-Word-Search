# WS16AI — Full-screen modular bookcase, Stage 1
**Date:** November 10–11, 2026 · **PR:** [#131 draft](https://github.com/PlayerEden/GridMates-Word-Search/pull/131) · **Status at source commit:** Android/art exact-source QA pending; artwork is an owner-review candidate, not release-approved.

## What was built, and why
- Replaced Full Library's isolated two small panels with a nearly full-width **two-tier cabinet** occupying the available space below its compact native header. Existing functionality—spine → full front cover/progress modal → View pages—is preserved, as are book IDs, saves, LAN and Home art.
- **HorizontalPager** shows complete cabinets only as the real book catalog grows. Slots per shelf are derived from measured available width and at least **48dp** per real book's independent touch target (54dp at large font). One cabinet holds two shelves; `pageCount = max(1, ceil(realBookCount / (2 * slotsPerShelf)))`. Native previous/next and a page counter appear only if two or more pages are needed.
- Every available cabinet slot is visually filled with a spine. Real catalog books come first, followed by recycled linen/night/burgundy **non-playable decorative volumes**. No fake catalog IDs, rewards, owned-book count, or onClick for blanks; the count still reflects only real books.
- The 3 existing V3 corrected themed spines and 3 existing blank spines are **alpha-cropped into a separate display sprite family** to give a physical near-touching row. Their trim retains their original aspect and painted geometry; no nonuniform stretch or cover-as-spine substitution.
- Added actual modular bookcase wood sprites: crown, left and right pilasters, recessed backboard, and base. They are generated deterministically from the **existing source-pinned real painted walnut shelf WS16AB**, plus the existing source-pinned WS16G plank, so painterly walnut material matches Home. The existing painted shelf renders independently as each level's ledge. Wood has **no printed text, book pixels or controls**. All parts and six spine exports plus manifest generated by `tools/asset_pipeline/build_ws16ai_bookcase.py` into QA-only `app/src/debug/assets/ws16ai/` and SHA256-verified in actual APK.
- Code paths: `BookcaseBookshelfScreen.kt` owns native layout/paging and book taps; `BookcaseWoodArtwork.kt` owns only local graphic layers and safe fallbacks; existing `BookArt.kt` keeps Home covers unchanged. Two independent CI workflows run new asset generator/test plus debug/release-isolation verification.

## Important creative and engineering limits
- This is **Stage 1** using transformed source-pinned painted walnut material as an authentic art basis; it is not a claim that the exact illustrated concept-board crown/pilasters have been perfectly hand-painted or split into new approved assets. Source master remains the original painted shelf; a later art-specific pass can replace any module without reworking UI.
- New wood/trimmed spine images are **debug-only**, and the normal release build has safe backboard/book fallbacks. Existing source spines and WS16AG APK are rollback. Home remains as before; **the optional one-cover Home Coming Soon teaser is deferred** until cabinet phone approval.
- Existing Android QA test tags for shelf, spine and preview are preserved where practical, but vertical scrolling assumptions are updated to horizontal paging.
- Responsive device acceptance still required at compact 320/360/412dp widths, tall and landscape, font scales 1.0/2.0, TalkBack, 0/1/3/7/12/13/24+ fixture books, Back and View pages, swipe + page buttons and saved progress.
- Do **not** count static screenshots or compiled source as confirmation of on-device interaction. No Play production release or merge without user approval.

## Validation gates
1. Source-check genuine painted walnut SHA256 and original rear wood SHA256 before image generation.
2. Source test validates 5 separate wood parts, 6 clean independent spine images, manifest and nonplayable placeholder behavior.
3. Android QA APK must contain **12 exact source-matched ws16ai files** (5 wood + 6 spine + manifest).
4. Independent debug/release art isolation must find those 12 images/manifest in debug and **zero ws16ai** in release.
5. Real phone shows cabinets fill screen, books are visually close and easily tappable, shadow/contact is natural, blank books do not navigate, and Space/Woodland/Garden correctly pull out.
6. Preserve known-good WS16AG as installed fallback until the owner approves this new cabinet.
