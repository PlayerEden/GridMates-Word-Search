## What changed

- The **actual letter grid** uses a **square, zero-radius paper frame and background**. No rounded shape on the board; the complete outer/corner cells display at Fit. Existing optical glyph centering, grid-line positions, touch hit geometry, 1×–2.5× zoom/pan and explicit Fit/Select controls remain unchanged.
- The list of words uses **straight rectangular print-style entries** instead of rounded pills. Labels can occupy up to two lines without ellipsis; each row's paging budget was increased to avoid the old cramped single-line allocation.
- Board-area side gutters increased from **10dp** to **16dp at screen widths below 360dp**, **18dp below 420dp**, **20dp below 700dp** and **24dp at 700dp+**. Margins apply outside the playable grid; no untested internal padding distorts letter positions or swipe coordinates. The layout recalculates the fitted square inside the available area.
- Pure test `PaperbackPageLayoutTest` covers gutter logic plus all four cell corners at Fit and at zoom 1.25×/1.5×/2×/2.5× for 8/12/15/20 boards. Android test `PaperbackViewportAndroidTest` checks a 320dp board's square dimensions/gutters/Fit and a genuine seeded-word touchscreen swipe.
- No new puzzle size or content generator. **12×12, 15×15, and 20×20 remain the current new Solo choices**. Versioned 25×25/30×30 is WS-09E, and continuous 100×100 is later research/development.
