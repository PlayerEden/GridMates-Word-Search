## Features actually implemented

1. In-play progress strip based on real found/target words; status feedback for found words and hints is clearer and politely announced to screen readers.
2. Responsive lined paperback word index, two columns normally, one at less than 350dp or at fontScale 1.3+; text labels remain scrollable with solved words visibly crossed off.
3. Dedicated completed Solo/Daily journal with real word count, elapsed seconds and hint steps. Only Solo offers **Play another puzzle**.
4. **Native Android share sheet** of aggregate result text. The share payload **does not** contain player name, device/room IDs, random puzzle seed, word answers, board layout or private records. Share failures are handled without crashing.
5. **Review completed board** displays the actual full solved puzzle and lets the player return to Results. Earlier save state, challenge/hint logic, board coordinate hit tests, multiplayer protocol v4, LAN guest recovery and original cosmetics are unchanged.
